About this demo

What this demo is, and is not (fictional data, browser-only storage, no voting or AI)
  • Everything seeded is fictional: people, expert roles, support, budgets, dates and commitments. No real institution is shown or endorses this.
  • It runs only in this browser. Nothing is sent to a community, a server or an analytics service.
  • What you type is kept in this browser’s local storage so it survives a reload. Local storage is not encrypted; anyone using this browser profile could read it. Avoid entering anything you would not want stored that way.
  • There is no AI, no sign-in, no voting and no check that participants are real or unique people.
  • Agreement is a clue, not a verdict. It can point to workable proposals. It does not prove something is true or representative, and it never overrides the needs of people who disagree.

Fictional pilot · Solid community

The pilot: make community tools easier to use

People who use Solid community apps describe what gets in their way, weigh three options for a small budget, and follow what the stewarding group decides and delivers.

The long-term idea is a federation of accountable communities, each with its own remit and people who answer for decisions. This pilot is one community with one small budget. Taking part is voluntary, and an open website only hears from people who choose to join, so what it hears is not representative.

Why every proposal names who decides

Gathering hopes changes nothing on its own. Every commitment here names the responsible body, its remit, the budget, a response date and a decision rule, and the body must publish its reasons.

How it works

  1. Describe a better day

    In your own words, privately first. You choose whether to share.

  2. Find related needs

    Read what others described and note what inspired you.

  3. Weigh real options

    Costs, trade-offs and who might lose out, including keeping things as they are.

  4. Ask the experts

    Experts advise on what is feasible. People affected set the priorities.

  5. Follow the decision

    Who decided, why, with what money, and whether it was delivered and evaluated.

          Export the public record

          The export is a versioned JSON file with stable record identifiers. It includes the proposal and options, every response and concern, the evidence and questions, commitments, any decision and the delivery trail, plus descriptions you chose to add to the demo community.

          It never includes your private drafts or saved bookmarks.

          Open the file in the separate read-only reader to see the same record rendered from the file alone. The reader shares this codebase, so it is a second view, not an independent implementation. This file format is not Solid or ActivityPub.
