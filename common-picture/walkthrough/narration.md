## Welcome {#welcome}

[do: chapter /]
[pause: 0.2]

[do: home-pitch]
The Common Picture is a standing, public picture of the lives people want. It's built from private conversations in people's own language, and tested by people drawn by lot.

[do: home-answer]
It asks institutions for one thing: answer it in public.

[do: home-strip]
This is a demonstration, on invented examples. Every person, host and number you'll see is invented. {@home-working-name}And "The Common Picture" is only a working name.

## The conversation {#conversation}

[do: chapter /listen]
[pause: 0.2]

[do: pick-language]
First, a private conversation about a real day. Choose a language, and a way in.

[do: begin-chat]
The interviewer says it's an AI, not a person. Then it asks about yesterday, not your opinions.

[do: example-answers]
We'll use invented example answers.

[do: type-money]
Now say something general, like "money".

[do: show-follow-up]
It doesn't argue, or give a view. It asks when money showed up in your day.

[do: finish-answers]
We'll answer the rest with examples.

[do: show-heard]
[do: change-and-drop]
Then it says: here is what I heard, in your own words. You keep, change or drop each point.

[do: show-consent]
Then you choose whether to add them to the picture for your place.

[do: add-points]
In this demonstration, nothing is stored.

## The picture {#picture}

[do: chapter /picture]
[pause: 0.2]

[do: picture-world]
The picture opens on the world layer, built from a hundred and sixty-two invented conversations.

[do: picture-layers]
You can pick a layer: five places, and one circle. {@picture-five-parts}Every layer has the same five parts, built upward, never by averaging.

[do: first-finding]
Part one is what's said almost everywhere. {@both-counts}Two counts travel with every finding: the share of people, and the share of places and circles.

[do: two-doors]
Counts come through two doors. The open door is anyone who chose to take part. The drawn sample is people picked at random.

[do: rests-on]
Nothing is reweighted quietly. This finding rests on the drawn sample.

[do: open-words]
Open it, and you see the original words, with labelled translations.

## Keeping difference {#difference}

[do: said-differently]
Part two, said differently, is the same need taking different shapes. Each statement is written from one side.

[do: standing-statement]
Part three, said here, keeps a standing statement whole. This one's from an invented Quechua-speaking community near Cusco.

[do: standing-framing]
It shows how one would appear. Real ones are written by communities, through their own process.

[do: in-tension]
Part four is in tension: a grower's fair price is a city family's food bill. {@not-yet-heard}And part five is who's not yet heard.

[do: open-challenge]
Every part has a button: this is not what was said.

[do: send-challenge]
In this demonstration, nothing is sent.

[do: retention-test]
Back at the top, a retention test checks that minority themes survive. Seven of eight do.

[do: open-kisumu]
Switch to Kisumu, and it's the same five parts for one place, {@kisumu-reply}with a right of reply from an invented traders' group.

## Talking across the world {#room}

[do: chapter /room]
[pause: 0.2]

[do: room-intro]
In a slow room, seven people drawn by lot work through one tension. {@room-days}It stays open for days, and it decides nothing.

[do: room-stand]
You write where you stand before you read the others. {@room-post}Then their messages appear in your language, with the original beside each one.

[do: talk-intro]
Talk it through is private. It gives no view of its own.

[do: talk-agree]
Say you agree, and it shows you reasons from people who answered differently.

[do: talk-what-true]
Then it asks what, if anything, is true in them for you.

[do: chapter /answers]
[pause: 0.2]

[do: answers-intro]
Institutions are asked to pledge to answer the picture in public, by a date. {@answers-notice}Every host here is an invented example. No real institution has pledged.

[do: answers-host]
Like this invented county government in western Kenya.

[do: answers-ask]

[do: answers-no-assembly]

[do: answers-register]
The register shows every answer, and counts the days when one is late. {@answers-check}And people drawn by lot check what was done.
