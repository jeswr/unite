The work uses Solid as the initial pilot assumption from the original brief. No community, funder, institution or expert has been contacted. The existing application is preserved. The prototype is an exploration of the proposed workflow, not a live participatory service.

The two supplied Claude URLs are authorization links, not saved conversations. The earlier work was found in a local checkout of [jeswr/unite](https://github.com/jeswr/unite), commit `0569e17`, and its design documents were used as reference. No prior Claude session was recovered from those links.

Before a real pilot, the two most consequential choices are **which community has a problem worth solving** and **who has the resources and authority to act on its decisions**. The prototype helps test the experience; the charter makes those commitments explicit.
